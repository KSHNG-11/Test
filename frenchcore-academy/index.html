Lab 1

Kick Lab

A kick synthesised live in your browser: a sine wave with a fast pitch drop (the punch), a held, tuned body (the tail), a noise click, and a distortion stage. The picture is the real waveform. The dashed line shows when the next kick lands, so you can see whether your tail fits.

Lab 2

Beat Lab

A 16-step grid, like FL Studio's Channel Rack: one row of 16 steps is one bar of sixteenth notes in 4/4. Steps 1, 5, 9 and 13 are the four beats. The kick uses your Kick Lab settings.

Tool

Tempo to milliseconds

Note lengths for any tempo. Use them for delay times, compressor release, or to check that a kick tail ends before the next kick (the 1/4 value).

Tool

Remix tempo planner

Enter the original song's tempo and your target. The planner compares stretching straight to the target with the half-time route (stretching to half the target, so one original beat lasts two of your beats).

Tool

Note frequencies

Equal temperament, A4 = 440 Hz, using the octave numbering where middle C is C4 (FL Studio's Piano Roll labels middle C as C5, so add one to these octave numbers there). Kick tails usually sit around the highlighted notes. Tap a row to hear a kick tuned to it and to send it to the Kick Lab.

Sources and further reading

The facts in the lessons were checked against these. Production tips (envelope times, frequency ranges) are common starting points, not rules: trust your ears and your reference tracks.

  • Frenchcore, Wikipedia: origins (Micropoint, founded by Radium and Al Core in 1992; Radium's label Psychik Genocide) and the modern melodic wave (Dr. Peacock, Sefa).
  • FL Studio manual: keyboard shortcuts: F8 Plugin Picker, F9 Mixer, Alt+F8 Browser, Ctrl+T typing keyboard to piano.
  • FL Studio manual: Fruity Limiter: sidechain routing and the COMP mode.
  • Mix Elite: how to sidechain in FL Studio: step-by-step sidechain routing.
  • MusicTech: FL Studio 21.2 adds Stem Separation: drums, bass, instruments and vocals; internet connection required.
  • Septum: frenchcore kick tutorial (.flp): an example of a layered kick (punch, "tok" layers, resonance, tail).
  • Hcontunes: frenchcore kicks with Serum: why volume envelopes come first.
